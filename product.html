How to Preserve Your Handmade Crochet:

  • Always spot-clean with a damp microfiber cloth and gentle baby shampoo or wool wash.
  • Do not bleach, machine wash vigorously, or tumble dry.
  • Reshape gently while damp and lay flat in shade to air dry.
